# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide engineering direction, ensure architecture alignment, and help the team make sound technical trade-offs while balancing delivery speed and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture guardrails for the project
- Review design decisions and guide implementation trade-offs
- Support developers with technical mentorship and issue escalation
- Coordinate dependency planning across services, integrations, and infrastructure
- Work with Product and Project Managers to assess delivery risks and sequencing

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce architectural drift and rework
- Improve engineering quality and delivery predictability

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review sessions and design discussions
- Weekly engineering syncs and planning meetings
- Technical risk updates and dependency reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers on implementation quality and technical decisions
- Partner with Product Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and trade-offs
- Supports Project Managers by highlighting delivery risks and dependency impacts
- Aligns with QA/Quality Leads on validation strategy and regression risk

---

## Release Managers / Deployment Leads

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the readiness, sequencing, and communication of releases to reduce deployment risk and ensure stakeholders are informed before and after production changes.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain release plans and deployment windows
- Coordinate rollback and contingency planning for critical changes
- Validate release readiness against quality, security, and operational criteria
- Communicate release status to stakeholders, support teams, and leadership
- Track post-deployment issues and follow-up actions

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and production disruption
- Improve visibility and predictability around releases
- Ensure consistent go-live readiness across projects

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists, readiness reviews, and launch briefings
- Status updates during deployment windows and post-launch reviews
- Incident and rollback coordination with engineering and support teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Project Managers on release timing and stakeholder communications
- Works with Technical Leads and Developers to confirm readiness and rollback plans
- Partners with QA/Quality Leads to confirm validation and smoke-test completion
- Supports Product Managers and stakeholders by communicating business impact and adoption timing

---

## QA / Quality Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Quality Leads ensure that the team validates outcomes, protects quality standards, and provides confidence that the delivered work meets defined acceptance criteria and user expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality and validation strategy for features, fixes, and releases
- Review acceptance criteria and ensure they are testable
- Coordinate test planning, regression coverage, and validation execution
- Identify quality risks, gaps, and release blockers early
- Partner with engineering and product stakeholders on issue triage and sign-off

### Goals
- Improve confidence in product quality and release stability
- Reduce defects reaching production and customer-facing environments
- Support predictable delivery with clear quality gates

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, validation reviews, and sign-off discussions
- Quality risk updates during sprint reviews and release readiness checks
- Defect triage and regression follow-up with engineering teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to validate implementation against acceptance criteria
- Aligns with Product Managers on customer impact and release quality expectations
- Supports Project Managers by identifying blockers, quality risks, and readiness gaps
- Collaborates with Technical Leads to validate critical workflows and integration risk

---

## Stakeholder Managers / Customer Liaisons

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Managers and Customer Liaisons maintain alignment between the project team and the people affected by or invested in its success, including sponsors, customers, support teams, and business leaders.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain stakeholder maps, communication plans, and updates
- Translate technical progress into business-relevant outcomes
- Surface customer feedback, concerns, and dependencies to the delivery team
- Coordinate expectation setting and decision requests across stakeholders
- Escalate unresolved risks or cross-functional issues when needed

### Goals
- Keep stakeholders informed, aligned, and confident in project progress
- Reduce confusion and churn caused by unclear communication
- Improve decision speed and stakeholder trust

### Typical Communication
- Status reports, milestone updates, and stakeholder briefings
- Feedback loops with customer teams, support, and business leadership
- Escalation summaries for risks, delays, or cross-functional blockers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to shape stakeholder-facing messages and business updates
- Collaborates with Project Managers on timing, communication cadence, and escalation paths
- Shares customer insights with Developers and Technical Leads to guide prioritization and quality decisions
- Supports Release Managers by coordinating communications before and after launch

---

## Support / Operations Partners

### Role Summary
Support and Operations Partners bring production readiness, incident handling, and operational continuity expertise to the project. They ensure that changes are reliable, observable, and supportable after launch.

### Responsibilities
- Review operational readiness and production support requirements
- Participate in incident response and post-incident learning
- Define monitoring, alerting, and runbook requirements for new features
- Help assess operational risks for launches, migrations, and support handoffs
- Coordinate support readiness and customer impact communication

### Goals
- Improve service reliability and resilience
- Reduce operational disruption during rollout and steady-state operations
- Make support and incident response easier for real-world users

### Typical Communication
- Operational readiness reviews and launch check-ins
- Incident summaries, runbook updates, and postmortems
- Support handoff and escalation communications during releases

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Technical Leads and Developers to ensure observability and rollback readiness
- Supports Project Managers in risk tracking and issue escalation for production impact
- Collaborates with QA/Quality Leads around smoke testing, validation, and launch criteria
- Shares customer-facing issues with Product Managers and Stakeholder Managers to guide follow-up
